# Homepage Experience Refinement

## Direction

The homepage should continue moving away from resume utility positioning and toward a lifelong, recruiter-aware career intelligence platform.

## Narrative Pillars

- Career Ladder grows with your career.
- Your experience may be more transferable than you think.
- Recruiter-aware guidance connects discovery, resumes, interviews, pathways, and tracking.
- The Master Career Profile becomes the source of truth for career outputs.

## Visual Direction

Maintain a calm, premium, editorial SaaS feel:

- Atmospheric depth
- Clear hierarchy
- Transformation storytelling
- Transferable skill examples
- Less card-grid utility energy

## Current Product Alignment

The site architecture already includes public landing pages for discovery, transitions, pathways, resume strategy, interview prep, mock interviews, master profile, and application tracking.

## Deferred

- More motion design
- Richer homepage transformation examples
- Dedicated offer comparison public page
- Public learning recommendation examples
- Full content hub expansion
