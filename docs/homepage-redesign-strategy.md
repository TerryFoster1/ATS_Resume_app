# Homepage Redesign Strategy

Created: 2026-05-27

## Positioning Goal

Career Ladder should no longer present primarily as a resume utility. The homepage should communicate a broader platform:

```text
Career intelligence for discovery, transitions, resumes, interviews, pathways, and long-term professional identity.
```

The emotional promise is that users may already have more relevant experience than they realize, and Career Ladder can help them understand, translate, and communicate that experience with recruiter-aware guidance.

## Narrative Structure

The homepage now leads with the career lifecycle:

- choose the career service needed
- add the role or opportunity context
- translate experience into hiring language
- build a reusable career workspace

This supports the goal-first onboarding model while keeping the existing workflows intact.

## Career Lifecycle Story

The "Every rung of the ladder" section frames Career Ladder around stages:

- Discover Your Direction
- Build Your Professional Identity
- Understand How Recruiters Think
- Transition Into New Opportunities
- Grow Throughout Your Career

The goal is to make Career Ladder feel like a long-term career platform, not a single document generator.

## Transferable Skill Storytelling

The homepage now gives transferable skill translation a visible product story.

Examples:

- chef -> operations coordinator
- retail manager -> customer success
- hospitality -> account management

This demonstrates that Career Ladder can interpret undervalued or adjacent experience and translate it into recruiter-readable language without inventing claims.

## Visual Direction

The visual system should stay:

- premium
- calm
- layered
- recruiter-aware
- editorial SaaS

The homepage uses existing Career Ladder surfaces and motion language, with added atmospheric depth and larger narrative zones instead of a generic feature grid.

## Intentionally Deferred

- no backend changes
- no auth or payment changes
- no generator changes
- no full app redesign
- no production dashboard redesign
- no CMS/blog system
- no new analytics implementation

Future work can deepen the visual system with richer product illustrations, but the current pass focuses on safe public-page positioning.
